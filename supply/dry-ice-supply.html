Dry Ice Ordering, Collection and Delivery

Find out what to include in a dry ice block order for collection or delivery in Kerala, from required weight to dispatch date.

Set the order weight and timing

Give the total weight, preferred block size if specified, and when the material will be used. Confirm whether you want to collect it or need a delivery to the packing site.

Prepare for repeat deliveries

List volumes by day or week and note any slow periods or shipment peaks. If you have several dispatch points, provide a delivery postcode and receiving contact for each.

Check logistics with your receiving team

Ask your logistics team what paperwork, packaging and handling requirements apply. Tell us if there are delivery access restrictions or a specific receiving window.
