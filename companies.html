Growth across Kerala

In 2020, KSIDC profiled Okay Group’s expansion from Kalamassery. The profile recorded oxygen operations in Thiruvananthapuram, Kollam, Kottayam, Kozhikode and Palakkad, acetylene operations in Kalamassery, and carbon dioxide operations in Udyogamandal, Kollam and Kozhikode.
