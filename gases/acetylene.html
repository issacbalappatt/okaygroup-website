Acetylene for fabrication

Dissolved acetylene is a fuel gas used in oxy-fuel fabrication. Buyers should specify their process, cylinder connection and the number of cylinders required. If you also need industrial oxygen, include it on a separate line so the two orders can be quoted clearly.

Cylinder availability

Specify whether you need a fresh supply or a cylinder exchange and where the cylinders will be delivered. For scheduled jobs, share the start date and whether the site has restrictions on delivery hours or access.

Purchasing documentation

Tell the team which product documents your site requires. Your equipment supplier or site safety team can verify the appropriate installation and handling requirements for your setup.
