Helium for instrument use

Helium can be used for analytical instruments and some leak-detection systems. The instrument or testing specification should identify the required purity. Include a copy or the exact grade and impurity limits in your enquiry so the team can check what can be sourced.

Details to put on the request

Provide the cylinder size or connection, required quantity and intended delivery date. Include your expected consumption if you need recurring supply. If the gas is part of a blend, write the complete mixture specification.

Grade and availability

Let us know whether your purchasing team needs a specification sheet or analysis documentation. Ask the team to confirm availability and lead time for the requested grade.
