Ask about gaseous nitrogen cylinders for purging, blanketing, fabrication or instrument use. Share your purity and cylinder requirements.

Request a quote

Match grade to the job

Identify whether the gaseous nitrogen is used for a plant process, a workshop operation or an instrument. Include the grade in the procedure and any stated limits for moisture or other impurities. For instrument use, attach or reference the gas specification.

State your cylinder configuration

Include the cylinder size, valve connection, quantity and any site requirements for an exchange. If a manifold or other site equipment is already in use, use its approved gas and cylinder specification in your request.

Estimate cylinder usage

Give the number of cylinders used in a normal week and during peak use. For an occasional job, state the quantity, site location and date the cylinders are needed.
