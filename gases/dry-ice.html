Dry ice for cold-chain packing

Dry ice is solid carbon dioxide. It may be specified for temperature-controlled shipments or other cooling applications. Ask about blocks and provide the required weight for each dispatch. If your packing process calls for particular block dimensions, include them in your request.

Book around dispatch

Dry ice sublimates into carbon dioxide gas. Coordinate the collection or delivery window with your packing schedule and have your logistics team confirm the packaging and transport requirements.

Repeat orders

If you ship regularly, provide the expected order frequency, typical volume and any seasonal peaks. State if you are packing at more than one site so the delivery team can check arrangements for each location.
