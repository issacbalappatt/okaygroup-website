Cryocans and tanks

Identify whether you need a portable cryocan or a larger storage vessel. Include the required capacity, intended application and whether there is an existing container on site. If replacing a vessel, a model reference or specification helps the team identify what to check.

Plan for refills

Share expected consumption, refill frequency and where the vessel will be received or filled. A container request and an ongoing liquid nitrogen supply request can be discussed together.

Documents to request

Ask for the vessel specification and any supporting documentation your facility needs before purchase. The responsible site team should confirm compatibility with its storage and receiving arrangements.
