Use the test specification

Identify the gas grade and impurity requirements listed by the leak detector manufacturer or on the test procedure. If the procedure specifies a mixture, include its complete composition and tolerance rather than quoting only the helium content.

Match the cylinder to the equipment

Specify the cylinder capacity and connection called for by the instrument or gas system. Include a make and model reference if the gas specification is available in the equipment documentation.

Coordinate with the test schedule

State whether the request is for a single inspection, a commissioning activity or routine production testing. Include the quantity, test date and the site contact receiving the cylinders.
