Welding and Cutting Gas Enquiries in Kerala

Ask about argon, carbon dioxide, industrial oxygen and dissolved acetylene for fabrication shops across Kerala.

Shielding gas

Shielding gas depends on the welding process and material. Read the gas specified by the welding procedure or equipment supplier and tell us whether you need a pure gas or a mixture. Include the mixture composition if the procedure specifies one.

Oxy-fuel cutting

For an oxy-fuel enquiry, identify the oxygen and fuel gas requirements separately. List the cylinder sizes and valve connections called for by the equipment documentation and the quantity for each gas.

Workshop or project orders

Send the number of workstations, usual usage and expected start date. Tell us if the order is for delivery to a temporary fabrication site and who will receive it.
