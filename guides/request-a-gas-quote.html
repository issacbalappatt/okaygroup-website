How to Request an Industrial Gas Quote

A purchasing checklist for industrial gas enquiries, including grade, cylinder size, quantity, delivery postcode and documentation.

Write one line for each product

Use the product name, grade, form and quantity. For example, the requested details for a nitrogen cylinder purchase include the grade, cylinder size and number of cylinders. Use your process or instrument documentation to fill in the specification.

Add the site and schedule

Provide the delivery postcode, desired first delivery date and how frequently you expect to order. For an existing arrangement, note whether you use an exchange or own the cylinders. For recurring orders, give a typical and a peak volume.

Include any supporting paperwork

List what your procurement team needs with the quote. For several sites, give each site a separate line with its quantity and location.
