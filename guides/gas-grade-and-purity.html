How to Specify Gas Grade and Purity

Learn what to include when ordering an industrial, laboratory or medical gas grade, from purity to required product documentation.

Use the application specification

Copy the required gas grade from the equipment manual, welding procedure or purchasing specification. If it gives both a minimum purity and maximum amounts for specific impurities, include both. For a blend, list each component and tolerance if specified.

Keep application and grade together

A gas name alone may be insufficient for purchasing. A production process, laboratory instrument and healthcare facility can each call for a different specification or documentation even if they use the same gas name.

State the supply format

Add whether you need a cylinder, liquid supply or a solid product. Include quantity and the documentation your facility needs before deciding on a supplier.
