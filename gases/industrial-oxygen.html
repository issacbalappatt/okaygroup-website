Oxygen for metalworking

Industrial oxygen supports combustion in processes such as oxy-fuel cutting. Metal shops should identify the process, existing cylinder arrangement and expected usage when ordering. This helps the supplier confirm compatibility and determine how often the shop needs replenishment.

What to include in an oxygen enquiry

Send the oxygen grade or specification on your purchase order, the cylinder size or valve connection your equipment requires, and the number of cylinders you need. If your facility already holds cylinders, mention whether you need an exchange or a new supply arrangement.

Industrial and medical grades
