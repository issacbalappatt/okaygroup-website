Laboratory, Analytical and Research Gas Enquiries

Enquire about laboratory gases in Kerala, including nitrogen, argon and helium. Share instrument specifications, purity and cylinder format.

Start with the instrument documentation

Copy the gas specification used by the instrument or procedure, including purity and maximum impurity levels if stated. For an instrument installation, the make and model and connection requirements help identify the appropriate enquiry.

Identify the grade for each use

An analytical instrument, an inerting step and a cooling process may need different grades or forms. Keep them on separate lines in the request. Include your anticipated usage when you need regular replenishment.

Cryogenic requirements

For liquid nitrogen, provide the volume per fill and any container requirements. If the laboratory needs a cryocan, include its required capacity in the enquiry.
