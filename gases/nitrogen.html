Gaseous nitrogen

Nitrogen is used in applications such as purging, inerting and blanketing. For cylinder enquiries, include your required purity, application and approximate monthly usage. Instrument buyers should supply the specification from their equipment documentation, including any impurity limits.

Liquid nitrogen

Liquid nitrogen is used for cryogenic cooling and some freezing applications. Tell us the amount per fill, refill frequency and what container your site uses. If you also need a cryocan or storage tank, make that part of the same enquiry.

How to specify the order

Identify whether you require gas or liquid and use the same unit for the quantity and expected consumption. Include the site postcode and your requested first delivery.
