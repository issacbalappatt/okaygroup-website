Industrial Gases for Manufacturing and Metal Fabrication

Plan gas supply for manufacturing and metal fabrication in Kerala, including industrial oxygen, nitrogen, argon and dissolved acetylene.

Cutting, welding and process requirements

Start with each workstation or process. Oxy-fuel cutting, welding and gas purging have different specifications. Use the gas named on the process documentation and flag cylinder connections or purity requirements that differ across the site.

Estimate replenishment

Collect consumption figures by workshop or shift. Average use helps establish a recurring order and peak use helps the team discuss stock for large fabrication jobs. Identify shutdown or project dates when a larger order may be needed.

Prepare one purchasing list

Send a separate line for each gas and grade. Put your delivery postcode, receiving contact and access requirements on the same request.
