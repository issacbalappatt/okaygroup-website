Bulk and Cryogenic Liquid Gas Supply Enquiries

Discuss liquid nitrogen, liquid argon and liquid carbon dioxide enquiries for sites in Kerala. Include consumption and receiving arrangements.

Describe the site and liquid requirement

State the gas requested, expected volume and required frequency. If there is already storage or receiving equipment at the site, share its capacity and any supplier requirements documented by the site team.

Compare one-off and regular supply

A one-off order needs a usable delivery window and clear receiving information. A recurring requirement also needs normal and peak usage estimates, so the team can discuss an arrangement that suits the site.

Provide a contact for technical details

Include the name of the person responsible for the receiving installation. For cryocans or liquid nitrogen tanks, include the vessel requirement alongside the liquid enquiry.
