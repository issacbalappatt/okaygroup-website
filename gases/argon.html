Argon Gas Cylinder & Liquid Argon Enquiries in Kerala

An inert gas commonly used to shield welds in TIG and MIG processes. Enquire about commercial, high-purity or liquid argon, depending on your specification.

Request a quote

Argon for fabrication

Argon is a shielding gas used in welding. The gas requirement can differ by welding process and material, so cite the gas or mixture on your welding procedure when you enquire. Include your cylinder format and how many cylinders the shop uses in a typical week.

Argon for instrument use

Laboratories and instrumentation teams may need a grade specified by their equipment manufacturer. Include the instrument make and model, minimum purity and any listed impurity limits. Commercial and high-purity argon are separate purchasing specifications.

Cylinder or liquid supply

State the form you need and whether the order is a one-off or recurring requirement. For liquid enquiries, also give the expected volume and existing on-site storage details.
